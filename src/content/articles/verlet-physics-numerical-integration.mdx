When building physics engines for video games, graphics simulations, or astronomical modeling, computer hardware cannot continuously integrate physical differential equations. Instead, physics engines approximate motion using **numerical integration** over discrete time steps $\Delta t$.

---

## 1. Summary & Key Takeaways

- **Explicit Euler Method**: Updates position using current velocity:
  $$x_{t+\Delta t} = x_t + v_t \Delta t, \quad v_{t+\Delta t} = v_t + a_t \Delta t$$
  - **Flaw**: Accumulates truncation error over time, causing physical systems to artificially gain energy and explode!
- **Verlet Integration**: Evaluates position from current $x_t$ and previous $x_{t-\Delta t}$ frame positions:
  $$x_{t+\Delta t} = 2x_t - x_{t-\Delta t} + a_t \Delta t^2$$
  - **Advantage**: **Symplectic & time-reversible**, accurately conserving total mechanical energy (Potential + Kinetic).

## 3. Mathematical Foundations

Verlet integration is derived directly by adding the forward and backward Taylor expansions of position $x(t)$:

### Forward Taylor Series ($t + \Delta t$)
$$x(t + \Delta t) = x(t) + v(t)\Delta t + \frac{1}{2}a(t)\Delta t^2 + \frac{1}{6}b(t)\Delta t^3 + \mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$$

### Backward Taylor Series ($t - \Delta t$)
$$x(t - \Delta t) = x(t) - v(t)\Delta t + \frac{1}{2}a(t)\Delta t^2 - \frac{1}{6}b(t)\Delta t^3 + \mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$$

### Adding Both Equations (Eliminates Velocity & Odd Terms!)
$$x(t + \Delta t) = 2x(t) - x(t - \Delta t) + a(t)\Delta t^2 + \mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$$

Because the $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^3)$ term cancels out, Verlet integration achieves **4th-order local accuracy** without calculating velocity vectors directly!

## 4. Integration Method Comparison Matrix

| Method | Order of Accuracy | Symplectic (Conserves Energy)? | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Explicit Euler** | $1^{\text{st}}$ Order ($\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$) | No (Gains Energy) | Basic UI animations |
| **Verlet Integration** | $2^{\text{nd}}$ Order ($\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^2)$) | **Yes** | Molecular dynamics, cloth & ragdoll physics |
| **Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4)**| $4^{\text{th}}$ Order ($\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$) | No (High Precision) | Orbital mechanics & aerospace trajectories |
